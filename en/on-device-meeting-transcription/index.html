On-device transcription

On-device meeting transcription. Your audio never leaves your device.

On-device meeting transcription means the speech-to-text runs entirely on your device, with no audio sent to a server. mind² ECHO transcribes meetings live with NVIDIA’s Nemotron speech model, sized to your phone, the words appear while people are still speaking, and once the meeting ends a second on-device pass tells the speakers apart, so a recording of a private conversation never has to travel to anyone’s cloud.

What on-device transcription means

Most meeting tools upload your audio to their servers to transcribe it. On-device transcription does the opposite: the model that turns speech into text lives in the app and runs on your device’s own processor. The audio is read and transcribed right there, and it stays on the device, kept locally so you can replay it, share it, or re-transcribe it later. Nothing about the recording, not the audio and not even the raw transcript, has to leave the device for the words to appear.

How mind² ECHO transcribes locally

Transcription runs on NVIDIA’s Nemotron speech model through the open-source sherpa-onnx stack. High-end phones run the full model; mid-range and low-end phones run a distilled, 12-layer version we trained from the original on about 2,100 hours of audio, which needs about a third less memory and runs roughly twice as fast. The choice is automatic and can be changed in the settings. Nemotron is a streaming model, so you watch the transcript build as people speak; it understands German and English, detects the language automatically, and transcribes faster than real time on a mid-range phone. Speakers are told apart in one pass after the meeting, by a dedicated diarisation model, NVIDIA’s Streaming Sortformer, which assigns every word to the right speaker and uses a pyannote segmentation model with wespeaker speaker embeddings to tell the voices apart. All of it runs offline on the device.

Why local transcription matters for privacy

A meeting is one of the most sensitive things you can record: salaries, strategy, health, personal conflict. Keeping transcription and speaker separation on-device means that sensitive audio is never exposed to a third-party server, a subprocessor, or a data breach you would never hear about. When you ask the room to agree to a recording, you can honestly say the audio stays on your device. Only the transcript text, never the audio, is later sent for AI analysis, and only with your explicit consent; the server does not keep it.

Works for in-person meetings, no bot required

Because mind² ECHO records through your device’s microphone, it works around a table just as well as on a video call. There is no meeting bot to invite into the call and nothing for the other participants to install. One tap starts the recording; the transcript and the per-speaker analysis follow. You can also import an existing recording, a FLAC or WAV file for example: the same on-device pipeline transcribes it faster than real time, with the transcript appearing live while it processes.
