On-device meeting transcription. Your audio never leaves your device.

The speech recognition runs with NVIDIA’s Nemotron speech model directly on your device, and your audio goes to no server. The free tier covers recording, transcription, import and speaker separation.

The app runs on iOS 18+ and Android 13+.

Speech recognition on your device

NVIDIA’s Nemotron speech model runs in the app on your device, through the open-source sherpa-onnx stack. The audio never leaves the device, because the speech recognition reads and transcribes it there. After the recording, NVIDIA’s Streaming Sortformer separates up to 4 speakers, also on the device.

What does leave the device

On the first start, the app downloads the speech models from GitHub. GitHub sees the IP address of your device, but no audio and no text. The optional cloud analysis is a paid add-on. It sends the transcript text with your consent through our server to AI services, and the server does not store it. The audio stays on your device in every case. The free tier sends no meeting content to a third party.

Free and without an account

The free tier includes recording, transcription, import and speaker separation for up to 4 speakers. The app sets no time limit on any of them. You need no account and no email address.

Record in the room or import

mind² ECHO records through the microphone of your device, so it works in a room with several people. No meeting bot joins a call, and nobody else has to install anything. You bring an existing audio file into the app with ‘Import Recording’.

Ask first anyway

On-device transcription does not remove the duty to ask everyone first. Recording the non-publicly spoken word of another person without permission is a crime in Germany under § 201 StGB. The law provides for up to three years in prison or a fine. Tell everyone involved and get their agreement before you record. This page is not legal advice and covers German law only.
